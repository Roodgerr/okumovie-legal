You can request deletion of your Okumovie account and the account data associated with it.

How to delete your account

  1. In Okumovie, open your profile, choose Delete account, and confirm. Firebase may ask you to sign in again before it deletes the account.
  2. If you cannot use the app, email us from the address registered to your Okumovie account. Include the account email and state that you request account deletion.
  3. Do not send your password or verification codes. We may reply to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

What is deleted and what remains

Okumovie deletes the Firebase Authentication account and account information associated with it. Okumovie does not store recordings, imported videos, editing projects, or gesture journals in that account or upload them to an Okumovie server. Those files remain on your device or in shared device storage and must be removed separately in Okumovie, Android, or your gallery. Google/Firebase may retain limited security or service records under its own retention practices.
