Privacy policy

Screen recordings, audio, imported videos, edits, gesture journals, and drafts are processed on your device. New recordings are saved to Movies/ProRec; private working copies and drafts are stored in Okumovie’s app storage. The app does not automatically upload these media files or synchronize projects to a server. Phone or gallery backup settings are controlled by Android and your chosen provider.

When you register or sign in with email and password, Firebase Authentication receives and verifies those credentials; Okumovie does not store the password. With Google sign-in, Google provides account details such as your email, display name, and profile photo when available. The Firebase SDK keeps a sign-in session on your device until you sign out. Firebase may also process security data such as your IP address and client information under Google’s privacy terms.

Okumovie currently has no in-app advertising or analytics SDK. A video leaves the device only when you choose a sharing or export destination that sends it elsewhere. That destination’s provider then handles the video under its own terms and privacy policy. The app picker reads launcher-app names locally so you can choose an app to record; this list is not sent by Okumovie to our server.

If you request a password reset, Firebase sends a recovery email to the address you enter.

Permissions and accessibility service

Android asks you to approve screen capture for each recording. Audio is optional and depends on the source you select and Android permissions. Overlay, microphone, notification, and vibration access are used only for the corresponding recording or control features and can be managed in Android settings.

If you enable the Okumovie accessibility service, it receives your touch input during a recording session and can replay the gestures you explicitly make. It may use window-change events to check whether the selected app is in the foreground; it does not retrieve or read window, screen, or text-field contents. The local session journal can include gesture timing and touch positions, plus the selected app’s package and window type; it is stored in private app storage for editing and troubleshooting. This policy does not replace the separate in-app disclosure and consent shown when the feature is enabled.

Account, deletion, and contact

You can sign out or request deletion of your Firebase Authentication account from the profile screen. Deleting the account removes the authentication account through Firebase; it does not delete recordings in Movies/ProRec, imported files, or editing drafts on your device. Delete those files separately in the app or Android. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its private working data; exported gallery files may remain.

For account or privacy questions, data requests, or complaints, contact rust0626@gmail.com. Where privacy laws apply, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal data, and to complain to your local privacy authority. Firebase handles authentication information under Google’s own retention and privacy practices.
